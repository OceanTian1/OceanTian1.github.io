Introduction

About Me

I am currently studying BSc (Hons) Geophysics at the University of Edinburgh. My academic interests include satellite remote sensing, geographic information systems, environmental monitoring and geospatial data analysis.

I have experience using ENVI, ArcGIS, QGIS and Google Earth Engine to process and analyse remote-sensing imagery. My project experience includes satellite-image preprocessing, temporal analysis of vegetation and water indices, and flood inundation monitoring.

I am also developing my programming and machine-learning skills using Python, with the aim of applying computational methods to geophysics, Earth observation and remote-sensing problems.
