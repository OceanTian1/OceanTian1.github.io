University Coursework

Absolute Gravity Estimation and Uncertainty Analysis

I compared four free-fall models using FG5-108 observations from BIPM, progressively including laser modulation, the gravity gradient and light-propagation time. The full model shifted the gravity estimate by −91.14 μGal relative to the constant-gravity baseline.

I analysed 1,193 repeated gravity estimates using residual time series, autocorrelation, histograms and Q–Q plots. The standard deviation was 12.48 μGal; the standard error of the mean was 0.361 μGal under an independence assumption.

Optical Remote Sensing

Sentinel-2 NDVI Time-Series Analysis and Median Compositing

I scripted a 2020 Sentinel-2 surface-reflectance workflow in Google Earth Engine using COPERNICUS/S2_SR. I filtered scenes by region and scene cloud cover below 40%, calculated NDVI from B8 and B4, and computed per-image regional means.

The workflow constructs a temporal median NDVI composite clipped to the study area and configures GeoTIFF export at 30 m sampling in EPSG:4326. The statistics are per image rather than daily aggregates; export completion has not been verified.

SAR Workflow Prototype

Sentinel-1 Flood Mapping with Backscatter Change Detection

I prototyped a Google Earth Engine workflow using Sentinel-1 GRD VV imagery in IW mode on descending passes. The script compares a 1 June–1 July 2021 baseline window with a 2 July–1 August 2021 event window using median composites and a 50 m focal-median filter.

The coded classification combines a backscatter decrease greater than 2 dB with event VV below −16 dB, excludes JRC permanent water with occurrence ≥90%, and sums pixel areas at 10 m sampling. The workflow remains a prototype under validation; no validated flood-area or accuracy result is reported.

Ongoing Research

Nearshore Small-Ship Detection in SAR Imagery

I am developing a ResNet–FPN detector with feature refinement using the HRSID dataset. Image preparation includes resizing to 800 × 800 pixels, random horizontal and vertical flips, and input normalisation.

I am investigating a two-branch risk-prior architecture: a standard RPN and RoI detection pathway alongside risk-map gating and feature refinement for classification.

I compare detections with ground truth using IoU, precision and recall. Failure analysis distinguishes risk-map coverage, proposal generation, box localisation and score ranking, particularly in cluttered nearshore and port environments.
